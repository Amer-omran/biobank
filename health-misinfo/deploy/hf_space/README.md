# Health Misinformation Detection — research prototype

Bilingual (Arabic RTL / English) research platform. **Research predictions only — not medical advice
and not definitive fact-checks.** Until a trained and evaluated model is uploaded, the app shows an
explicit "model not trained" state.

Open the app at its direct URL (`https://<user>-<space>.hf.space`) so that sign-in cookies work.
Arabic fact-checked claims: AraFacts (Sheikh Ali et al., 2021), CC BY-NC 4.0.
